Privacy policy

1. Controller

The controller under the GDPR is Tavleen Kaur, Feelhomestays, Fichardstraße 54, 60322 Frankfurt am Main, Germany, email: frankfurtkey@gmail.com.

2. Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc., San Francisco, USA. When you open a page, GitHub processes technically necessary data (IP address, date and time, page requested, browser and operating system) to deliver and secure the site. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the EU standard contractual clauses apply in addition.

3. Booking requests

When you send a request through the “Book direct” page, we process your name, email address, optional phone number, reason for the trip, chosen space, dates, number of guests, price and your message. The data is processed through the form service Web3Forms (Web3Creative, Kerala, India) and sent to us by email. The purpose is to handle your request and perform the accommodation contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We delete the data once it is no longer needed and no statutory retention periods under commercial and tax law apply.

4. Payments

Where we offer online payment, it takes place on the payment providers’ own pages: Stripe Payments Europe, Limited (Dublin, Ireland) for card payments and PayPal (Europe) S.à r.l. et Cie, S.C.A. (Luxembourg). We pass on the space, dates, amount, name and email address; we never receive card or account details. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. The providers’ privacy notices also apply.

5. Availability calendar

The site shows which nights are taken. To do this we retrieve calendar data for our listings. No personal data of guests is displayed.

6. Google Analytics and Google Ads

Only with your consent do we use Google Analytics and Google Ads, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Cookies are set and usage data is processed (pages viewed, device and browser information, approximate location, identifiers and booking events with their value) to analyse use of the site and measure our adverts. The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and section 25(1) TDDDG. Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Retention in Google Analytics: 14 months. You can withdraw consent at any time through “Cookie settings” in the footer.

7. Local storage

We store your cookie choice in your browser and briefly keep booking details in the session during a payment. This is technically necessary (section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

8. Fonts

Fonts are loaded from our own server. No connection to Google Fonts is made.

9. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR), and the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Der Hessische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit), Wiesbaden.
